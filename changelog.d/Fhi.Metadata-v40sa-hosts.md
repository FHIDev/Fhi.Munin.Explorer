category: Notes for hosts
- **The contents nav is a `<details>`, and a new name, `munin-explorer-page__toc-count`.** The nav's heading and a count of its entries now sit in the `<summary>` of a `<details>` drawn open; the list follows it. The browser module shuts it below 768px and keeps it open, with the summary out of the tab order, from 768px. Without the module the list stays open. A matching Stiler rule lays the summary out on one line on a phone and hides the count and the arrow from 768px; until a host has the Stiler release carrying it, a wide screen shows «Innhold (N)» with the browser's own arrow.
