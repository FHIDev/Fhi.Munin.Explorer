category: Added
- **A reader who is not signed in can keep a variable list in their browser, behind a feature flag.** With `MuninExplorer:Features:AnonymousLists` on, the save button in `VariableSearch` is offered to a signed-out reader and adds the row to one list kept in the browser's local storage; the list tab shows it, downloads it in the formats a saved list offers, shares it as a code (while the snapshot stays under the share endpoint's 512 KB, about 990 full rows), and empties it after a confirmation. Each row holds catalogue fields only: `variabelId`, `variabelCode`, `variabelName`, `kildeId`, `kildeName`, `kildeKortNavn`, `datasamlingId`, `datasamlingName`, `variabelgruppeName`, `dataType`, `dataFrom`, `dataTo` and `versjonStatus`; at most 2000 rows. Signed in with rows still in the browser, the list tab offers «Lagre som ny liste i Mine lister», which saves them as a new list and then takes those rows out of the browser; nothing is carried over without that press.
- **`IMuninExplorerClient.ExportItemsAsync` exports a list nobody has saved, one line per (variable, datasamling).** It is the supported successor to the obsolete `ExportListAsync`, on the same anonymous route, and carries a default body, so a host implementing the contract itself still builds.
