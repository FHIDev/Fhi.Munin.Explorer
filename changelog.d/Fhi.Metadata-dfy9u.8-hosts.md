category: Notes for hosts
- **Three new class names, `munin-explorer-save-all`, `munin-explorer-data-list__item--saved` and `munin-explorer-data-list__saved-notice`, are styled from `Fhi.Helsedata.Stiler` 0.1.152; run that or later.** `munin-explorer-save-all` wraps «Lagre disse variablene», its confirmation, status and alert under the result count; the samples give it `margin: 12px 0 16px`, and without a rule the confirmation sits tight under «Kolonner» below 375px. For three seconds after «Lagre disse variablene», each newly saved row's `<li>` carries the modifier, and its row holds the notice «Lagret i liste <name>», which is `aria-hidden`. The sample stylesheets lay the notice over the row's columns in helsedata's cyan, keep «Fjern fra liste» painted above it, and fade it out. Without rules the notice is a line of text under the row's columns for three seconds.
- **A collapsed result row's save cell holds its own error again.** A failed press on the row's «Lagre i liste» is said in a `role="alert"` inside `munin-explorer-dataitem-main__save`, in the `munin-explorer-data-list__save-status` the open panel already uses. The cell is 10rem wide in Stiler, so a sentence there wraps over several lines and makes that row taller until the next press.
