- **Stiler 0.1.219 styles the saved list's import notice and its «Historisk» mark.**
  `munin-explorer-list-import`, `munin-explorer-list-import__entry` and `munin-explorer-list-status`
  have Stiler rules from 0.1.219, so a host on it no longer needs rules of its own for them. A
  host on an older Stiler still does, as the earlier notes say.
