  `munin-explorer-list-import`, `munin-explorer-list-import__entry` and `munin-explorer-list-status`
  have rules in Stiler 0.1.219, the version helsedata pins, so a host on it needs none of its own
  for them. A host on a Stiler without those rules still does, as the earlier notes say.
