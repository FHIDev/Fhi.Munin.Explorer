category: Added
- **A list create or add refused at Munin's per-person list quota now says so.** Munin refuses a new list once a person holds 50, and an add that would take their lists past 100 000 rows together, with a 409 naming `list-limit` or `row-limit`. The new list form, a copy, a saved shared list, a datasamling chosen for a row, the search's save button, «Lagre disse variablene» and the signed-in carry-over of the browser list now tell the reader the limit and what to do («Du kan ha høyst 50 lister. Slett en liste før du lager en ny.»), in their language, instead of saying that saving failed. A copy cut short by the row limit says it is incomplete and then the limit. The sentence is the package's own, worded as Munin's, because these writes send no `Accept-Language`.
