- **The saved-list view's buttons stand in one line under the list picker, with the rest behind «Flere valg».** The action row holds the list picker, «Legg til ny liste», «Last ned» (was «Last ned listen», shortened so it shares a line with «Flere valg» at 320px) and a «Flere valg» button. The button folds out Gi nytt navn, Del liste, Kopier liste, Tøm liste, Slett listen and Åpne delt liste. The picker stands on a line of its own with its label above it, and the buttons form a line under it. Before, up to nine controls in three styles took two rows at 1280px and five at 320px; now the buttons are one line at 1280px and two at 320px, under the picker. «Last ned» is now the same kind of fold as «Flere valg», a button with a panel, where it was a `<details>` whose controls stood in a row. Choosing a form from «Flere valg» closes it and puts focus in the form's field. Escape closes either fold and returns focus to its button. Delete and empty still ask for confirmation inside the fold, and closing the fold cancels a confirmation that is waiting. The row is now drawn even when it holds no picker and no download.
