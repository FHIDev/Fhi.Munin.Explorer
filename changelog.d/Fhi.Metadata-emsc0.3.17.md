- **Saving the browser list to Mine variabellister from two tabs at once makes one list, not two.** A signed-in reader who pressed «Lagre i Mine variabellister» in two tabs before the first had taken the rows out of the browser got two lists of the same rows. The first press now holds a short claim in the browser, and a press in another tab, or in a second list view on the same page, that finds it makes no list and says the list is already being saved; its rows stay in the browser until the first press has moved them. A claim left by a tab closed mid-press lapses after 90 seconds.
