category: Changed
- **A kilde and datasamling above a chosen variabelgruppe are half-ticked in the kildefilter.** Choosing a variabelgruppe under a datasamling in the variable explorer's kilde tree now marks that datasamling and its kilde as partly chosen, the way helsedata.no's own filter shows it, without choosing them: the results stay narrowed to that datasamling's group. Screen readers hear "(1 valgt under)" ("(1 chosen below)" in English) on each of them. The half-tick is set by `explorer-interop.js` after render, so it needs the package's JavaScript module; without it the words still say it. No new class name.
