- **«Søk om tilgang» on the kilde page.** The action row says how to get the kilde's data, chosen by its Tilgangsnivå. «Søknad via helsedata.no» gets a «Søk om tilgang» button to the host's application form and links to the two application guides, naming the dataansvarlig. Otherwise «Tilgang krever søknad» links to the registry's own application page, its address or, failing both, its website. «Åpne data» or «Statistikkbank» adds a link to the kilde's open data, or to the host's open-data page. A kilde with no Tilgangsnivå gets nothing. The registry links come from Munin's new kilde fields SoknadOmTilgangUrl, SoknadOmTilgangEpost and AapneDataUrl (Munin 1.39.0). (Fhi.Metadata-l9l2n.142)
