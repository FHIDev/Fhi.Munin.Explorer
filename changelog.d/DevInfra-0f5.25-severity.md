- **REVIEW.md: severity is judged by likelihood and impact** - A new «Judging severity» section, guidance weighed case by case and not a hard rule (Robin 6.11, ask:0mw4pa5ng000): Important when ordinary use can hit a defect, when data is lost, corrupted or shown wrong, when someone gets access they should not have without an administrator acting oddly, or when a claim users or integrators rely on is false; a Nit when it needs several unlikely conditions, an administrator acting oddly, or leads only to a harmless outcome. The kinds the file names as Important stay Important however unlikely, judgement still decides, and a rating against the guidance says why. (DevInfra-0f5.25)
