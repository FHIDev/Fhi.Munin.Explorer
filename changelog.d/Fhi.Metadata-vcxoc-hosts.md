category: Notes for hosts
- **A `KildeView`, `VariableView` or `DatasamlingView` mounted on its own makes one `/api/explorer/filters` request per language.** It goes through the registered `IMuninExplorerClient` and nothing else is needed. Inside `VariableSearch`, `VariableListView` and `KildeSearch` an opened view shares the explorer's answer — the facets it already holds, where it has them — rather than asking again for every row it opens. A host that mounts `VariableView` or `DatasamlingView` without registering a client still renders them, with the kildetype word shipped in this package; `KildeView` needs the client as before, for its hierarchy.
