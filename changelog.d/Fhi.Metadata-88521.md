- The sample hosts' stand-ins for Stiler's `headline-xxs` and `headline-s` were the wrong size, so
  the samples under-stated what the component looks like on helsedata.no. Measured there on
  2026-09-02, `headline-xxs` is 16px/160% and `headline-s` is 21px/160%; the samples drew them at
  14px/150% and 20px/150%, which put a group heading and a `dt` field label two pixels *under* the
  16px value beside them. A host outside helsedata's estate that took its rules from the samples
  should take the corrected sizes with them. The weight stays the sample's own — Graphik is
  licensed and not redistributed here, so 500 stands in for the `graphik-medium` face their
  declaration names, the way the `headline-3` stand-in above it already does. (Fhi.Metadata-88521)
