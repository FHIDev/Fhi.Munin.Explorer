category: Notes for hosts
- **Two new names for the wide-screen filter fold: the button `munin-explorer-filters__collapse` and the panel modifier `munin-explorer-filters--collapsed`.** The button is a direct child of `.munin-explorer`, beside the panel; a host shows it only where the panel is a sidebar and, while the modifier is on, hides the panel and gives the results the panel's column. The sample stylesheets do both from 1024 px; Fhi.Helsedata.Stiler owes the same rules. Without them the button shows on a phone beside «Vis filtre» and pressing it changes nothing on screen. The choice is kept in `localStorage` under `munin-explorer.filters-collapsed`.
