category: Notes for hosts
- **Three new class names, `munin-explorer-list-menu`, `munin-explorer-list-menu__panel` and `munin-explorer-list-menu--end`, need Stiler's rules.** They are the «Last ned» and «Flere valg» folds in the saved-list view's action row; `--end` marks «Flere valg», which stands at the end of its line and, from 768px up, opens leftward; below 768px both panels are as wide as the row. The panel is drawn with `hidden` while the fold is closed, so a host stylesheet that gives it a `display` must also honour `.munin-explorer-list-menu__panel[hidden] { display: none; }`, or the fold stands open. Without any rules, a panel is an in-flow block under its toggle whose buttons sit side by side and wrap, and it still works. The picker's `<label>` in the action row now takes a line of its own as a column in Stiler (`.munin-explorer-page__actions > label`), and that rule also reaches a `<label>` a host passes in a detail view's `Actions`.
- **Run `Fhi.Helsedata.Stiler` 0.1.149 or later for the saved list's action row.** On 0.1.148 and earlier the picker shares its line with the buttons, the folds have no chevron, and an opened panel pushes the rows below it down instead of lying over them.
